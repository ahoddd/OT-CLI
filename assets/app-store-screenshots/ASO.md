# App Store Optimization (ASO) — OrbTap

## Screenshot suggestions

Capture these screens for store listing and social:

1. **Map with orb pins** — Home/Map tab showing partner orbs, discovery.
2. **Scan / earn flow** — Scanner HUD with "Earn 30–80 OT" and success state.
3. **OrbSwipe deck** — Swipe-through venue picks for planning your night.
4. **Leaderboard podium** — Local Legends with ranks and streaks.
5. **Premium badge** — Plans screen with 7-day free trial and benefits.
6. **Wallet + Points to Perk** — OT balance and Silver/Gold/Platinum ladder.
7. **Partner page** — Venue detail with perks, social proof ("X visited this week · X OT redeemed").

## Rating prompts (in-app)

Trigger "Enjoying OrbTap? Rate us!" at delight moments:

- After first verified scan
- After completing first mission
- After reaching a new level (e.g. Scout → Explorer)

Use `expo-store-review` (or store deep link) so the prompt opens the native review dialog when available.
