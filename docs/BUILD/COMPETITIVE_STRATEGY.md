# OrbTap — Competitive Strategy: Feature-by-Feature "Competition Killers"

**Goal:** Make every OrbTap feature clearly better than top competitors without breaking the app or drifting from our identity (proof-backed, local, real rewards).

**OrbTap moat:** Verified visits + proof + missions that drive real foot traffic. Competitors either lack verification, lack real rewards, or lack the local-business mission.

---

## 1. Map & Local Discovery

| Competitors | What they do well | OrbTap outdo tactics |
|-------------|-------------------|----------------------|
| **Google Maps** | Coverage, search, directions | We're not a map replacement; we're "orbs at real places" — emphasize **verified partners only**, **rewards on tap**, **mission orbs** (gold) so the map is a rewards layer, not a generic directory. |
| **Yelp / Foursquare** | Reviews, lists, tips | We don't rely on anonymous reviews; we have **proof of visit** and **verified actions**. Copy: "Real visits. Real rewards. No fake check-ins." |
| **Punch / Grubbl** | Loyalty, discovery | We combine **map + missions + proof + wallet** in one flow. Mission strip + "View on map" + OrbSheet "Part of your mission" = one tap to intent. |

**In-app:** Map tab subline or legend: "Verified spots · Tap orbs for rewards" so the differentiator is visible without changing behavior.

---

## 2. Daily Ritual (Tap the Orb / Streak)

| Competitors | What they do well | OrbTap outdo tactics |
|-------------|-------------------|----------------------|
| **Duolingo** | Streak, notifications, habit lock | We're not language — we're **daily ritual that pays in real-world value** (OT Points, streak rewards). Copy: "Shatter the orb. Earn rewards at real places." |
| **Streaks / Habitica** | Habit tracking, RPG skin | We give **real currency** (OT Points) and **real perks** (redeem at partners). No fantasy gold — real redemption. |

**In-app:** Daily ritual section title or subline: "One ritual. Real rewards." or "Shatter the orb — earn OT Points you spend at local spots."

---

## 3. Missions

| Competitors | What they do well | OrbTap outdo tactics |
|-------------|-------------------|----------------------|
| **Generic "daily tasks" apps** | Checklists, reminders | Our missions are **location-based** and **partner-backed** — "Lunch at X", "Dinner at Y" with real businesses. Copy: "Missions that send you somewhere real." |
| **Step / fitness challenges** | Steps, challenges | We drive **foot traffic to businesses** and reward with OT + Sphere XP. Good for you + good for local. |

**In-app:** Missions hero: "Visit local spots. Earn rewards." is strong; add one line: "Proof-backed. Real places. Real perks." to beat generic task apps.

---

## 4. Wallet & Points

| Competitors | What they do well | OrbTap outdo tactics |
|-------------|-------------------|----------------------|
| **Punch / loyalty apps** | Punches, free item after N visits | We have **unified OT Points** + **verified actions only** (no fake check-ins). Copy: "Points from verified visits and redemptions only." |
| **Cashback / credit rewards** | Percent back | We're **local-first** — spend points at partners, not abstract "rewards catalog." |

**In-app:** Wallet subline or earn-next: "Earned from verified visits & redemptions" so trust is explicit.

| Competitors | What they do well | OrbTap outdo tactics |

## 7. Scan & Redeem / Proof

| Competitors | What they do well | OrbTap outdo tactics |
|-------------|-------------------|----------------------|
| **QR loyalty / Punch** | Scan to punch, redeem later | We have **instant proof** (proof card, shareable) + **verified action** on-chain of record. Copy: "Scan. Prove. Share. Verified local win." |

**In-app:** Proof screen and scan success already say "Verified Local Win" — keep that; ensure share card and success screen reinforce "proof-backed."
